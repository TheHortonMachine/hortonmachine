# GeoframeInputsBuilder

Prepares, from the rasters of a basin and of its sub-basins, what the models of the GeoFrame modelling environment need: the geometries of the sub-basins and of the network, their topology, and their attributes. In the [ERM workflow](../geoframe/erm/ermdatapreparator.md) it writes them into the GeoFrame database.
